The Feast/Famine Cycle Has an Energy Root

Most agents think the feast/famine cycle is a pipeline management problem. It isn't. It's an energy broadcast problem.

When you're overwhelmed — deals stacking, problems multiplying, more than you can handle — your energy broadcasts "I have too much. I cannot take on more." And the universe, your sphere, your referral network — they all respond accordingly. The leads slow down. The calls decrease. The feast ends.

Then when the famine hits and anxiety rises — the desperation energy broadcasts "I need business. I need a deal." And clients feel that need radiating off you in every interaction. Which pushes them away. Which deepens the famine.

The agents who break out of the feast/famine cycle learn to broadcast a different signal entirely: genuine gratitude for the business they have, combined with a genuine desire for more — not from desperation, but from confidence that they have real value to offer and more people to serve.

That signal — gratitude plus service-minded desire — is the one that creates consistent, sustainable production. And it can only be broadcast from a clear, recovered, regulated nervous system.
